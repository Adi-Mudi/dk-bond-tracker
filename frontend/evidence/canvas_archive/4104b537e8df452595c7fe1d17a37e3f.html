Data Sources & Market Feeds

Standardized Ingestion

Primary settlement calendars, ISIN coupon frequency tables, and sovereign benchmark yields adhere to standardized exchange reporting mechanisms without non-disclosed data adjustments.

NSE (Wholesale Debt & CBRICS) Daily Curve

National Stock Exchange (NSE Wholesale Debt Market / CBRICS) - Daily sovereign yield curves, Government of India (GOI) dated securities, and Treasury bill benchmark cut-offs.

BSE (Bond Reporting Platform) Corporate Master

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE Bond Reporting Platform) - Corporate Non-Convertible Debenture (NCD) master listings, ISIN coupon schedules, and official credit agency rating actions.

Depository CAS (NSDL & CDSL) SEBI Compliant

Depository CAS Ingestion (NSDL & CDSL) - Direct client-side structured parsing of e-CAS consolidated statements according to standard SEBI circular formats.
