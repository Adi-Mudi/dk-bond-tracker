Ledger Isolation & Cryptography

This App & Your Data

lock

All bond records, bank references, and cashflow projections are stored locally on your device or private vault. No financial credentials, PAN records, or banking passwords leave your machine.

sensors_off Zero Telemetry

No tracking beacons, analytics SDKs, or cloud telemetry. The application functions in fully air-gapped environments.

key Client-Side AES-256

Stored balances, yield maps, and broker references are encrypted at rest inside client IndexedDB using custom passphrases.

receipt_long Read-Only Ingestion

Parser executes solely inside browser memory for NSDL/CDSL Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) without persistence to external APIs.

terminal

Offline ledger calculations compute coupon timelines, XIRR, and clean/dirty pricing deterministically using client-side WebAssembly arithmetic.
