Q5 RULES —

Hidden rules (written note)

BUSINESS LOGIC
•
Monthly plan formula: target = saved + monthly × months + portfolio growth (needs start + end dates) · e.g. ₹11,50,000 + 22 mos × ₹35k + ₹80k growth = ₹20,00,000
•
Entry exclusivity: target-OR-monthly exclusive entry (non-entered side shows as derived read-out) · goal name required
•
Preservation invariant: linked bonds never deleted with goal (watchlist pattern)
•
Removal gate (2026-11-27): active goals cannot be removed — Archive only; Remove unlocks at 100% achieved (guard: type full goal name)
•
Urgency tier hierarchy: Behind > Due Soon > On Track > Completed (dim)
