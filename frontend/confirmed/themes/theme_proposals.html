5 Static Comfort Themes — pick 3

Combined research: my web research (calm-palette science) + AGY CLI consultation (Gemini, WCAG-verified token math). Constraints honored: static only · no dynamic engines · no flashy fintech/neon · low saturation · no pure #000/#FFF · comfortable for long sessions.

10 candidates (R1 ×5 + R2 ×5 non-white) live preview per theme full token sets all ≥ WCAG AA 4.5:1 (AGY math check) ✓ LOCKED ×5: Nordic Mist · Quiet Slate · Harbor Navy · Warm Ledger · Deep Fjord

Research basis (both reports combined)

My web research — comfort science

  • Low-saturation backgrounds reduce anger/arousal (2025 behavioral study, multiple hue families)
  • Soft off-whites (#f5f5f0) beat pure white; near-blacks beat #000 — less harsh max-contrast
  • Pure black = OLED halation (smearing on scroll); pure white = glare — both banned
  • Restrained palettes cut cognitive load (NN/g) — when everything shouts, users freeze
  • Warm neutrals (#F5F0EB) = the 2025–26 reaction to cold corporate white (Notion-adjacent)
  • Finance trust = desaturated blues/greys, never high-chroma "ticker" colors

AGY consultation — verification layer

  • Every token in all 5 themes math-checked: ink/muted/primary/semantic ≥ 4.5:1 on each surface layer
  • on-primary checked against primary too
  • Zero pure #000000 / #FFFFFF surfaces in any theme
  • Each theme carries one honest risk (not just praise)
  • AGY shortlist pick: Evening Umber (warm-dark, lowest blue-light/eye strain)
  • Spread enforced: 2 light · 2 dark · 1 warm-dark

How to choose — side-by-side

  • 1 Warm Ledger — best daylight/paper feel; light + traditional
  • 2 Nordic Mist — best modern light; cool, airy, table-friendly
  • 3 Quiet Slate — calmest evolution of your current dark; familiar shift
  • 4 Evening Umber — least eye strain overall (AGY pick); distinctive, warm, night-friendly
  • 5 Pine Charcoal — unique organic dark; green-tinted, grounding

Suggested spread if you want variety: 1 light + 2 darks + 1 warm-dark (e.g. 2 + 3 + 4), or all-dark (3 + 4 + 5) if you never leave Midnight-style. Static tokens only — whichever 3 you pick, they slot into app_theme.dart as alternate palettes, same component styles.
