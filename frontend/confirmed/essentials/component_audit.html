Method & rules applied checklist

7 questions per component: ① Data (displayed + source: parent vs self-fetch) · ② Actions (component-owned gestures only, with direction + conflicts) · ③ States (working/loading/empty/error — even if the page designed them) · ④ Feedback (toast/undo/confirm/haptic — never wonder if it worked) · ⑤ Rules (validation/permission/business) · ⑥ Connection (self-fetch vs parent-passed) · ⑦ Screens (phone/tablet/desktop reflow).
Gesture rules: declare supported AND unsupported · accidental swipes safe (no destroy on one swipe) · system gesture wins conflicts · long-press destructive → confirm first.
Nesting: each level runs its own checklist · page never talks to the database — only to its components.
