You entered the NET credit; the rate above decides the split — standard 10% gives gross = net / 0.9 and TDS = net / 9, exempt gives gross = net and TDS = 0. TDS applies to interest only (s.193), never to principal. The deducted amount is your claimable tax credit (Form 16A) — declare it in your ITR. Official TDS deposits round to the nearest Rs 10 (s.288B); this app shows exact whole-rupee values for clarity.
