Backup

Not connected Backup is off. Nothing is uploaded, and the app makes no network call for backup until you connect.

The sign-in opens in your browser, never inside the app. On Android it comes back through a link — Google does not allow its consent screen to run inside a WebView.

We ask for openid, email and drive.appdata — the hidden app folder in your Drive that only this app can read. Nothing else in your Drive is visible to us.

Connect Google Drive (opens browser)

You can disconnect at any time. Disconnecting removes the stored token and stops every backup; your local data is untouched.

What happens after you connect

Nothing is sent until you press Back up now or set a time. The first backup after connecting asks you once.
