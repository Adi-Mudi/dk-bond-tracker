Move to Portfolio — Alpha Securities Ltd

INE9000A01001 · watching → holding
Why two dates: the bond's interest cycle starts on its dated date (issuer-set), while purchase date is when you bought — they differ on secondary-market buys (settlement T+1). Tenure is auto-calculated from start → end date, as today. Quantity × face value gives the bond's maturity value; invested amount is what you actually paid (with quantity recorded separately, per-bond cost and totals can both be tracked).
