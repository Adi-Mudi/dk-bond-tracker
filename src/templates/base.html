    Why bonds?

    Fixed, predictable returns. That is what bonds are for — you know the coupon and the schedule up front. The share market can move a lot in either direction; bonds do not promise equity-level gains and are not built for them.

    Expectation check: if you are looking for more than roughly 10–12% returns, that is the share market's territory — not bonds. Choose bonds for fixed returns; choose equity only if you accept its risk for higher expectations.
    Flip side: if you have been putting daily effort into shares and still are not beating 10–12% — or you are losing — bonds deserve a serious look. Fixed returns, and you only need to analyse a few things once, not watch the market every day.
