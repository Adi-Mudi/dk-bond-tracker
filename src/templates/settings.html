Stored only on this device (local database) — never uploaded. Use an App Password, NOT your login password. Leave blank to keep the saved password.

Setup guide
  1. Gmail: enable 2-Verification on your Google account.
  2. Create an App Password at myaccount.google.com (search "App Passwords").
  3. Paste the 16-char app password into the SMTP password field — NOT your login password.
  4. Outlook / Microsoft 365 personal accounts use the same app-password concept.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong port — use 587 with TLS/STARTTLS, or 465 with SSL.
  • TLS required — most providers refuse plain connections.
  • Check the spam folder — test emails often land there.
